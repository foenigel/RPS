Welcome to the Rock-Paper-Scissors game! Here’s how to play:

Select Your Gesture: Click on one of the three hand gestures: Rock, Paper, or Scissors. Rock beats Scissors (Rock crushes Scissors) Scissors beats Paper (Scissors cuts Paper) Paper beats Rock (Paper covers Rock) Go: After selecting your gesture, click the "Go" button to submit your choice. Play Against the PC: The computer will randomly select its gesture. Determine the Winner: The game will announce the winner based on the rules: If you and the PC choose the same gesture, it's a tie! If your gesture beats the PC's gesture, you win! If the PC's gesture beats yours, you lose!
